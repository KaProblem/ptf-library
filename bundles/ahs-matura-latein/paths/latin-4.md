# Introduction

This course prepares for the standardised written Matura in Latin
(4-year) at an Austrian AHS. Learners who started Latin in the 9th school year (5. Klasse) and take the 4-year Latin Matura. The exam lasts 270 minutes and has two
parts: a translation text (36 points) and an interpretation text with ten
tasks (24 points); a dictionary and the official prefix/suffix list are
allowed. Each step teaches what one group of checkpoints or task formats
asks for, with questions in the style of the exam; every answer that can
be checked objectively is recomputed by a script. It ends with a complete
practice exam. It takes about 25 hours of course work, plus practice exams under time.

# Prior knowledge

Four years of school Latin cover all the grammar, but often thinly: forms of the 3rd declension, deponents, the gerundive and the subjunctive clauses are typical gaps. Start with the `probe` question of each element; steps where every objective is shown can be completed as placement, but phases B and C deserve real practice for most 4-year learners.

# Tutor notes

- Points first: the translation part is 60 % of the exam and needs 18 points on its own; sense units (12 points) reward understanding, so always ask "what happens here?" before details.
- Grammar first, but always in sentences: after each element of phases B and C, translate a short passage and mark it with SE, LE, MO and SY checkpoints.
- Interleave: from phase C on, start each session with five minutes of forms from phase B.
- The interpretation tasks 1–4 (loanwords, word formation, sentence analysis, quotations) are the cheapest points; drill them until they are quick.
- Past Matura papers are not part of this course; when the learner brings one (photo or PDF), guide them through it step by step instead of solving it for them.
