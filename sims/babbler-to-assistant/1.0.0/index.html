Station 3 · Estimate

How much bigger?

A small model that writes Shakespeare-like text has about 10 million parameters (adjustable numbers). GPT-3 has 175 billion. Roughly how much bigger is GPT-3?

Imagine the small model is a single sheet of paper. Then GPT-3 would be a stack of 17,500 sheets: almost two metres high, taller than a grown-up.

The bars are logarithmic: each step to the right means ten times more. The blueprint of both is almost the same, a decoder-only transformer. The difference is size, amount of data and compute.
