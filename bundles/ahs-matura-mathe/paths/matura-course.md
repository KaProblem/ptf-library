# Introduction

This course prepares for the standardised written Matura in mathematics at
an Austrian AHS. Each step is one chapter of the official catalogue of basic
competences; the questions are written in the style of the Matura answer
formats (2 aus 5, Lückentext, offen) and every computed answer is checked by
a script. At the end the learner can solve Teil-1 tasks of every area and
combine them in Teil-2 context tasks.

It takes about 30 hours of course work, plus mock exams. Technology
(GeoGebra or a graphing calculator) and the official formula sheet are
allowed in the exam and should be used in the course.

# Prior knowledge

Most learners preparing for the Matura have met every chapter in school.
Start with the `probe` question of each element to find what already sits;
steps where every objective is shown can be completed as placement. Vectors
(C) and statistics (E) hardly depend on analysis and can be alternated with
it.

# Tutor notes

- Prioritise by points: probability, vectors, rates of change, linear functions, functions and equations together bring about 13 of the 24 Teil-1 points.
- Interleave: after each analysis step, mix in a few questions from an earlier chapter.
- Always ask for units and the meaning in context; many Matura points are lost on interpretation, not on calculation.
- Past Matura tasks are not part of this course; when the learner brings one (photo or PDF), guide them through it step by step instead of solving it for them.
