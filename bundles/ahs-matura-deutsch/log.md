# Log

- 2026-11-05: Version 0.1.0. Bundle created: 12 elements (exam format, assessment grid, argumentation, structure and style, language accuracy, and the seven text types Zusammenfassung, Leserbrief, Kommentar, Erörterung, Meinungsrede, Textanalyse, Textinterpretation), 77 questions, the learning path `matura-course`. Every text type has a full original writing task with a rubric along the four dimensions of the SRDP assessment grid and a sample text. `checks/` recomputes the numbers and checks that every sample text fits its word range, uses only figures from its Textbeilage and quotes it word for word. The official SRDP documents could not be fetched from the cloud environment; format and grid were taken from search results and secondary sources and should be compared with the current documents on matura.gv.at before the element status is raised. Blueprints for new writing tasks: general rules in exam-format, one blueprint per text type (operators, situations, Textbeilage, rubric template) in the tutor notes, so the tutor can build unlimited new tasks and theme packages in the exam format.
- 2026-11-06: Aligned with the official SRDP documents (Textsortenkatalog 2020, Operatorenkatalog, Beurteilungsraster from 2022/23 with Handreichung 2024, Korrektur- und Beurteilungsanleitung 2025/26, Schularbeitsleitfaden), which the maintainer provided; the documents are paraphrased, not copied. assessment-grid: five levels instead of four, the 15 criteria, competence areas K1 to K3 with balancing in K3, automatic "nicht erfüllt" cases, the threshold for year grades and the text-length rules; new question `k3-balance` (78 questions). Text types: Kommentar may be written by a student or citizen and avoids „ich“; Leserbrief needs an opening and a closing formula (sample answer now opens with an address) and has only 270–330 words; Erörterung has no situation and three argument patterns; Zusammenfassung never evaluates and is always 270–330 words with a situation; Textanalyse and Erörterung 405–495 or 540–660; rubrics of all writing tasks now follow the grid criteria. exam-format: the official operator list, one operator per instruction, three to four instructions, area III never first, Textbeilagen at most about 2,000 words per package. Two task bullets reworded to single operators.
