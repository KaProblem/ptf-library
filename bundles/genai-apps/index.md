# Contents

A course on building applications with large language models, written
for learners who want to use and build with generative AI rather than
study the network inside (that is the bundle llm-basics). It covers what
a model does and costs, prompting and sampling settings, chat
applications, embeddings and retrieval-augmented generation, function
calling and agents, image generation, the choice between prompting, retrieval and
fine-tuning, and responsible use, security, user experience and
evaluation. Every element has a highschool and a college level text,
a `probe` question for placement and `check` questions for every
objective at both levels.

Scope and order follow Microsoft's open curriculum Generative AI for
Beginners (MIT License), without its vendor-specific lessons (cloud
services, low-code tools, single model families). Text, examples,
questions and figures are original. Prices in examples are illustrative
($2 and $8 per million input and output tokens), not quotes.
