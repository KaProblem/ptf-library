# Contents

A course for learners preparing for the standardised written Matura (SRP)
in German at an Austrian AHS. The exam has no "chapters" like mathematics:
the candidate chooses one of three theme packages and writes two texts of
given text types. So the course first teaches what every text needs (the
exam format, how the texts are assessed, argumentation, structure, style and
language accuracy) and then the seven text types one by one.

The explanations are in English, as everywhere in this library. Everything
the learner reads or writes in the exam is in German: the writing tasks
(Schreibaufträge), the source texts (Textbeilagen), the sample texts and
the language exercises. German exam terms are kept in German
(Erörterung, Arbeitsauftrag, Textbeilage).

All writing tasks, source texts and sample texts are original. Past Matura
tasks are not part of this bundle (they are published by the BMBWF without
an open licence); a learner can bring one and be guided through it. The
one literary text, Franz Kafka's "Gibs auf!" (1922), is in the public domain.

Every writing task has a rubric whose lines follow the four dimensions of
the official assessment grid (Inhalt, Textstruktur, Stil und Ausdruck,
normative Sprachrichtigkeit). `checks/` recomputes the numbers (word ranges,
time plans) and checks that every sample text has the length its task
demands.
