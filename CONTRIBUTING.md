# Contributing

Contributions come as pull requests, from people or from agents working for
them. Automated gates review them (see
[CONTENT-POLICY.md](https://github.com/KaProblem/ptf-spec/blob/main/CONTENT-POLICY.md) §2).

## Your responsibility

You are responsible for what you contribute. You must hold the rights to it
and comply with copyright and all other applicable law (trademark, privacy,
personality rights). The automated checks help catch mistakes; they do not
take this responsibility off you.

Use any material as a source (books, courses, videos, papers), but write the
content yourself: no copied or closely paraphrased text, no translations, no
exercises or figures taken from a source. Only CC0, public domain, CC BY or
CC BY-SA material may be adapted, with author, title, link and license in
`sources`. Details: CONTENT-POLICY.md §1.1.

If an agent prepares the contribution, you are still the contributor.

## License of your contribution

By opening a pull request you license your contribution under this
repository's licenses ([LICENSE](LICENSE)):

- content in `bundles/`: CC BY-SA 4.0
- simulators in `sims/` and tools in `tools/`: MIT

## Contributor statement

Every pull request contains this statement (the PR template has it). By
submitting, you confirm it:

> I created this contribution myself or have the right to submit it. It does
> not copy text, questions, figures or code I may not license under this
> library's licenses, and it complies with applicable law. I license it under
> the licenses in this library's LICENSE file.

## Reporting a problem

If you believe content here infringes your rights or the law, open an issue.
Maintainers remove or fix infringing content without delay and may take
disputed content down while they check.
